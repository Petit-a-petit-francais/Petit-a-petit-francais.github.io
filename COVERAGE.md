**Totals:** 238 verbs × 8 tenses, 615 practice sentences, 3,421 words in 29 themes, 39 grammar lessons with reference sheets.

**Vocabulaire progressif, chapter by chapter:** every index word for all 28 chapters (Les usages … Les arts – les spectacles) is on the site, and each chapter's lesson pages were scanned for words missing from the index (≈210 added, e.g. chaleureux, prétentieux, tousser, frisé, l'entrecôte, rembourser, le jour férié, applaudir).

**Not imported:** Persian translations (they extract as garbled text), and the textbooks' reading passages and exercise answer keys.

**FrenchMoMo A1 Notes + A2 : 1–15, page by page (441 pages):** every French word on every page was checked against the site's words, all verb forms, sentences and rule sheets. Image-only pages were OCR'd (page 260, a COD/COI worksheet); the others are term covers or blank. What remains unmatched is only names, English glosses, the teacher's stem lists (boi-, buv-…), inverted questions (parles-tu), and spelling slips in the notes (acceuil → accueil, ascendre → monter, chirirugien → chirurgien, virlangue → virelangue, souhaterions → souhaiterions).
