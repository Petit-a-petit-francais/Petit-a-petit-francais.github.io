Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Petit à petit français is a free French practice app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and we don't collect any personal data.

Your progress stays on your device

Your answers, review schedule, lessons and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. If you use Export progress, the file is saved on your device and goes only where you put it. Erase all progress in Settings deletes it, as does clearing this site's data in your browser. So the app works offline, your browser also keeps a copy of the app's own files (not your data) on your device; clearing site data removes that too.

Read-aloud voices

Words and sentences are read aloud by the voices built into your browser or operating system. Most of them work on your device. Some, such as the Google voices in Chrome and the "Online" or "Natural" Microsoft voices in Edge, are cloud services: when you use them, the text being read is sent to Google or Microsoft to make the audio, under their privacy policies. To keep everything on your device, choose a voice in Settings that isn't a Google, Online or Natural voice.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when the site loads, and may log them for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app makes no other network requests: fonts and icons are served from the same site.

Children

The app doesn't ask for or collect information about anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/Petit-a-petit-francais.
